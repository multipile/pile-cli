What a pile is, read from a repository's declarations: its config, its id,
name and path, the piles it sits in and holds, and its intent.

What belongs here is everything that follows from the declarations alone:
what a config may say and how one line of it is rewritten, what an
intent's headline is, which pile a path is in, what changed, arranged by
the pile it landed in, and what is wrong with any pile's config, intent or
place. What a pile's `.toml` files declare, and what its diagrams show, do
not belong here: each is read and checked on top of what is found here.
How a repository is read does not belong here either, and neither does how
an answer is printed or a file is written. Nothing here knows git, and
nothing here writes.

Read this pile to find out what makes a subtree a pile, which pile a path
is in, and what doctor holds a pile to. Change it when what a config or an
intent may say changes, when the rule that gives a path to a pile does, or
when doctor's verdict on a pile does.
