The diagrams a repository keeps in its piles' `.pile/diagrams/`: which
files they are, what doctor finds wrong with each, and how one is drawn as
a page or a picture.

What belongs here is what ties a diagram to the repository: finding the
files among the declarations, holding each to the rules of its format and
to the piles it names, and handing a diagram that reads to multipile's
diagrams to draw. What a diagram may say, how it is laid out, how it looks
and how it is rendered do not belong here: they are multipile's diagrams',
pinned in this crate's manifest. Neither does a command, or how an answer
is printed. Nothing here knows git, and nothing here writes.

Read this pile to find out where a repository's diagrams are and what is
checked of them. Change it when where a diagram is kept, what doctor holds
it to, or which commit of multipile's diagrams it is drawn with changes.
