The guide `pile guide` prints, compiled into the binary: where a session
begins, the workflows carried out from the top, and the reference pages
read when they apply.

What belongs here is each page, the section a repository keeps in its rules
file, and what a page is held to: its front matter, a workflow's parts, and
the tests that hold every page to them. A page says what holds across
commands. What one command takes and prints belongs in that command's help,
and how a page is printed as an answer belongs with the commands. Nothing
here reads a repository.

Read this pile to find out what a session is told, and the shape a page or
a workflow takes. Change it when a command a page names changes, or when a
workflow is added, changed or removed.
