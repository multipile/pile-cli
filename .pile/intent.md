What makes the piles here one release of `pile`: built with one toolchain,
put through one check together, and published together.

The root holds what every pile is held to alike: the toolchain, the check,
the release and CI, and the instructions a session here starts from.
Anything that serves only one pile belongs in that pile.

Read this pile to find out what a change has to pass before it is released,
and what every pile here shares. Change it when the toolchain, the check or
the release moves.
